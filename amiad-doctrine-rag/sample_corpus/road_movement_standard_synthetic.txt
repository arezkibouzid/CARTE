ROAD MOVEMENT STANDARD FOR LOGISTIC CONVOYS (SYNTHETIC)

[page 1]
1 Scope
1. This standard sets the rules for planning the road movement of logistic convoys in the theatre.
2. It applies to all convoys unless a national supplement states otherwise.

2 Definitions
1. A convoy is a group of vehicles moving under one commander.
2. A serial is a subdivision of a convoy that moves as a unit.
3. A halt is a planned stop of the convoy for rest, refuelling or maintenance.

3 Road classification
3.1 Classes
1. Roads are classified by carriageway width. Class A roads have a width of at least 6.0 m.
2. Class B roads have a width of at least 4.5 m but less than 6.0 m.
3. Class C roads are narrower than 4.5 m and are to be used only when no other route exists.
3.2 Use by heavy vehicles
1. Heavy vehicles shall not use roads narrower than 4.5 m.
2. Tank transporters should be routed on Class A roads whenever possible.

[page 2]
4 Speed
1. The maximum speed of a convoy on Class A roads shall not exceed 60 km/h.
2. Heavy vehicles shall not exceed 40 km/h on Class B roads.
3. In built-up areas the convoy shall not exceed 30 km/h.
4. The speed limit for Class C roads is given in Annex A.
5. At night the maximum speed should be reduced by 10 km/h.

5 Serial intervals
1. Serials shall keep an interval of at least 150 m between the last vehicle of one serial and the first vehicle of the next.
2. Within a serial, vehicles should keep a distance of 50 m on open roads.
3. The distance between vehicles shall be doubled in poor visibility.
4. The time gap between convoys sharing a route shall be at least 10 min.

6 Halts
1. A halt shall be made every 2 hours of driving.
2. Each halt should last at least 15 min.
3. Halt areas shall be located outside built-up areas.

[page 3]
7 Bridges and tunnels
1. A convoy shall not cross a bridge whose load class is lower than the MLC of its heaviest vehicle.
2. Tank transporters with MLC 100 shall use only bridges of MLC 100 or higher.
3. Tunnels shall have a clearance of at least 0.3 m above the highest vehicle.
4. Vehicles taller than 4.0 m shall not enter tunnels without prior reconnaissance.

8 Hazardous cargo
1. Fuel tankers shall avoid towns of more than 10 000 inhabitants.
2. Vehicles carrying explosives must not be halted within built-up areas.
3. Convoys carrying hazardous cargo shall be escorted.

9 Gradients and turns
1. Heavy vehicles shall not use gradients steeper than 12 %.
2. Sharp turns with a radius below 25 m shall be avoided by vehicles longer than 12 m.

10 Itineraries
1. Where several itineraries link the same two zones, they shall not intersect and shall be at least 5 km apart.
2. The primary itinerary should be the fastest one.

11 Incident reporting
1. Incidents shall be reported to the movement control centre without delay.
2. A convoy commander may change the route after an incident if the change is reported at once.

Annex A Speed limits for Class C roads
1. On Class C roads the convoy shall not exceed 20 km/h.
2. Heavy vehicles may use Class C roads only with an escort.
