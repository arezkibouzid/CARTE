NATIONAL SUPPLEMENT TO THE ROAD MOVEMENT STANDARD (SYNTHETIC)

[page 1]
1 Speed and spacing
1. Heavy vehicles shall not exceed 35 km/h on Class B roads.
2. The time gap between convoys sharing a route shall be at least 10 min.
3. A halt shall be made every 3 hours of driving.

2 Administration
1. The national movement control centre keeps the register of convoys.
2. Requests for exceptions shall be sent to the national movement control centre.
