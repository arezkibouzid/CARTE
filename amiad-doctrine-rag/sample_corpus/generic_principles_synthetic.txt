PRINCIPLES OF MOVEMENT AND TRANSPORTATION (SYNTHETIC)

[page 1]
1 Principles
1. Movement and transportation planning must be kept as simple as possible.
2. Plans should be flexible and responsive to changes in the operational situation.
3. Commanders must report significant delays to the next higher headquarters within 2 hours.
4. The exercise normally lasts 5 days and has historically involved 12 nations.
5. Transport capacity must be used efficiently to support the objective.
6. Road transport is flexible and can reach most destinations, but its carrying capacity over long distances is limited.
